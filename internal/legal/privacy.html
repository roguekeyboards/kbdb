Last updated: November 1, 2026

mykeebs is a place to keep track of your mechanical keyboards, switches, keycaps and builds. This policy covers all of mykeebs: the website, the API, and AI assistants connected through MCP. It's run by Rogue Keyboards. Questions or requests: support@mykeebs.com.

What we collect

When you sign in. You sign in with Discord, the only way to sign in. Discord sends your Discord user ID, username, display name, avatar and email address. Sign-in is handled by Stytch, which keeps these for us, along with the sign-in tokens Discord issues. mykeebs itself only uses the account ID Stytch gives you, and your email only to match a support request to the right account. The sign-in tokens Stytch gives the mykeebs website and apps can also carry your email address and name; mykeebs doesn't read or store them. Your mykeebs username and avatar are your own, separate from Discord's.

What you add. Your profile (username, preferences, avatar) and everything you put in your collection: keyboards, switches, keycap sets, builds, prices you paid, and photos.

Logs. Our servers log each request, including your IP address and user ID. We use these to fix problems and stop abuse.

Why we use it

We use your data to provide the service you signed up for, and logs to keep it running and secure. It's stored in the United States; if you're elsewhere, using mykeebs means your data goes there.

Who can see it

Your items are private unless you choose to make them public. Prices you paid are shown to others only if you allow it in your preferences.

Photo checks

Every photo you upload is checked automatically by Amazon Rekognition for inappropriate content. Photos that fail are hidden. We keep a rejected photo for 30 days, in case it was a mistake, then delete it.

Who else handles your data

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics. These services run parts of mykeebs for us:

  • Amazon Web Services: stores your data and photos in the United States, and serves photos through its CloudFront network, which keeps temporary copies in North America, Europe and Israel.
  • Stytch: handles sign-in.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and routes traffic.
  • Discord: only to sign you in.

Your browser

The website keeps your sign-in in your browser's local storage, and the sign-in page uses Stytch's cookies to remember your session. Nothing else is stored, and nothing is used to track you.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile and collection: until you delete them.
  • Your sign-in record at Stytch: until 30 days after you delete your account.
  • Server logs: 14 days.
  • Photo check records: 60 days.
  • Backups: deleted data can stay in backups for up to 35 days.

Your rights

You can edit or delete any item or photo yourself at any time. You can also delete your whole account yourself. This removes your profile, your collection and all your photos right away. Your sign-in record at Stytch is removed within 30 days. Copies in backups are gone within 35 days.

You can also ask us, at support@mykeebs.com from the email on your Discord account, to:

  • send you a copy of your data (your profile, collection and photos),
  • correct anything you can't fix yourself,
  • delete your account for you, or stop using your data in some way.

We'll answer within 30 days, and won't treat you any differently for asking. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

mykeebs is not for anyone under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For big changes, we'll post a notice on the site first.
